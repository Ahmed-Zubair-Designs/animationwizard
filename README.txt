ANIMATION WIZARD - HOW THE SITE WORKS

FILES
  index.html, portfolio.html  the two pages
  style.css, app.js           design and behaviour (don't edit)
  videos.js                   THE ONLY FILE YOU EDIT: videos, contact links, categories and prices
  logo.png, logo-ink.png      logos (logo-ink is the dark one used on the light design)
  favicon.png, me.png         browser icon and your photo

CHANGE VIDEOS, LINKS OR PRICES ANY TIME
  - GitHub > your repo > videos.js > pencil icon > edit > Commit changes.
  - The live site updates in about a minute.
  - The instructions at the top of videos.js explain each part.

SHARE ONE CATEGORY WITH A CLIENT
  - On the Portfolio page, right-click a category in the left panel and copy the link.
  - Example: https://animationwizard.site/portfolio.html#character-explainers
